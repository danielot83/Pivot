Settings

Only admins of a club can change these.

Club logo

Shown across Pivot wherever your club appears.

No logo yet
  • Square image, ideally at least 256 × 256 px — non-square logos get squeezed to fit.
  • Transparent background (PNG) works best — it'll sit correctly on any color. If your photo has a plain background, remove it first for free at remove.bg or remove-bg.io, then upload the result here.
  • Formats accepted: PNG, JPEG, or WebP. Max 2 MB.
  • Pivot resizes it automatically, so don't worry about the exact pixel size.

Members

Change someone's role, or remove them from the club. You can't demote or remove the club's last admin.

Exercise sharing

Share every exercise in your library with all other clubs on Pivot at once — they'll be able to see (not edit) them. You can still set sharing exercise by exercise in the Exercise creator.

Club association

A middle circle between "just my club" and "everyone on Pivot" — a small group of clubs (e.g. a league) who agree to share content with each other. Needs approval from the Pivot platform controller before it becomes real.

Already know the name of an existing association? Ask its members, then request to join it the same way — matching names request to join the same association instead of creating a new one.
