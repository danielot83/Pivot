Players by season and team — contacts, license status, jersey numbers, one place per club.
A club's roster changes every season — new players arrive, others move up an age group, licenses need renewing. Roster keeps all of that organized by season and by team, with a tree of your seasons and teams on the left instead of scattered spreadsheets.
Edit any player right in the table, no separate edit screen. Add a jersey number, license status, a player photo, and the license document itself. Choose which columns you see (add AVS number or birth year, hide what you don't need), and export or import a whole team from Excel in one click — it even reads common French club-export layouts automatically.
Build a drill by hand — court diagram, difficulty, tags — or fill it in with an AI prompt.
Every coach accumulates exercises over the years — drills for dribbling, shooting, defense, game situations. Create exercise gives each one a real court diagram, a difficulty rating, categories, and age groups, so you can find the right drill in seconds instead of digging through old notes.
Fill it in by hand, or copy a ready-made prompt into any AI chat, describe the drill, and paste the reply back — it fills in name, difficulty, categories, tags, objectives and description for you to review. The diagram itself always stays hand-drawn. Choose exercise by exercise whether to keep it private to your club or share it so other coaches on PlayPivot can use it too.
Browse, search, and filter every exercise — yours and whatever your club/association/community has shared.
Once you've got more than a handful of exercises, finding the right one again is the real challenge. The library lets you search by name, tag, or description, and filter by difficulty, age group, category, language, favorites, or ones you've never actually used in a session.
Exercises can come from your own club, from your club association, or from the whole PlayPivot community, each marked with its own little icon so you always know where a drill came from. Duplicate one to make it your own, or duplicate it as a translation to keep the same exercise in more than one language.
Pull exercises into a session plan, track total time, mark attendance, print the sheet.
Planning a session usually means picking exercises from memory and guessing at timing. The training builder lets you pull exercises straight from your library into a session plan, with a running total that compares against how long you actually have.
You can also mark who attended, straight from your roster, so attendance history builds up automatically over a season instead of living on a paper sheet.
Lineups, starters, live stats, and a season summary per player — generated as a clean PDF.
On match day, you need a call-up sheet, a way to mark starters, and a way to record what happens — points, minutes, fouls — for each player. Match day handles all three, pulling the roster in automatically so you're not retyping names.
Across a season, those individual match sheets add up into a per-player summary — games played, points, minutes, fouls, and averages — useful for checking that playing time is being shared fairly, not just match by match.
Score technique, IQ, personality and attitude by criteria, with automatic sector averages and a chart.
Beyond day-to-day coaching, tracking a player's development over a season (or several) needs a real framework, not just a gut feeling. This module scores 36 concrete criteria across 4 sectors — technique, basketball IQ, personality, and behaviour — each rated 0 to 5, modeled on real federation assessment sheets. A basic/advanced toggle keeps it simple for a first pass, with the more tactical criteria available when you want them.
Sector averages update live as a bar chart while you fill it in, with a legend explaining what each score means. Generate a clean PDF (with the same chart) or export a player's whole history to Excel, and use the separate Statistics tool to line up different players — even from different seasons — side by side.
Draw your team's sets and plays — offense, out-of-bounds, defense — not just individual drills.
The exercise library covers individual drills, but a team also needs its actual playbook — half-court or full-court sets, out-of-bounds plays, defensive schemes. This is a dedicated drawing board for that, using the same conventions coaches already know: solid arrows for cuts, a T-cap for screens, dashed lines for passes, wavy lines for dribbles.
Drag players to set the formation, draw the actions, then hit Animate to see the play move — useful for showing a team how a set actually flows, not just a static diagram. Save plays into your own playbook, tag them to a season/team, and export any of them as a clean PDF.
Training-connected team overview, player development trends, and side-by-side comparisons — all calculated live.
Numbers scattered across sessions, matches, and assessments don't help much on their own. Statistics pulls it all together live — sessions logged, minutes trained, attendance rate, and which categories your team actually works on most, straight from Training builder, no extra setup.
Pick any players, from any season or team, and see their development over time — not just their latest assessment, but a real trend line across every one they've ever had. Line up match stats and training attendance side by side too, filtered by season if you only want part of the picture.
