◐ What is Pivot

A coaching notebook that grew into a platform.

Pivot started as one exercise binder and turned into a full coaching workflow: a shared exercise library, session builders, match sheets, and a detailed player-development tracker modeled on real federation assessment criteria.

It's now moving online and free to use, so any coach — not just the club it was built for — can create an account, set up their own team, share it with their whole coaching staff, and use it from a phone, tablet, or computer, without installing anything.
