◐ What is Pivot

A coaching notebook that grew into a platform.

Pivot started as one club's exercise binder and turned into a full coaching workflow: a shared exercise library, session builders, match sheets, and a detailed player-development tracker modeled on real federation assessment criteria.

It's now moving online, so any coach — not just the club it was built for — can create an account, set up their own team, and use it from a phone, tablet, or computer, without installing anything.

What's inside

Roster

Players by season and team — contacts, license status, jersey numbers, one place per club.

Exercise library

Court diagrams, categories, difficulty — start from a shared base, or keep your own private set.

Training builder

Pull exercises into a session plan, track total time, mark attendance, print the sheet.

Match day

Lineups, starters, live stats, and a season summary per player — generated as a clean PDF.

Player assessment Suivi

Score technique, IQ, personality and attitude by criteria, with automatic sector averages and a chart.

Vector PDF sheets

Every exercise, session, match and assessment exports as a clean, printable PDF with your club's logo.
