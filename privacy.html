Part A — Terms of Use

A1.Who we are and what PlayPivot is

PlayPivot is a web application for running a basketball team or club: rosters, attendance, player assessments, match statistics, season planning, training sessions, exercises and plays. It is operated by (“PlayPivot”, “we”, “us”).

PlayPivot is under continuous development: features change, and parts of the service may be incomplete or unavailable at times. It is a coaching tool, not legal, medical, psychological or sports-governance advice — statistics, reports, objectives and assessments only reflect what you and your club enter.

A2.Accepting these terms, and who may use PlayPivot

By creating a PlayPivot account, you agree to this document as it stands on the date you accept it. We keep a dated, versioned record of that acceptance — an actual log entry: who, when, which version — not just a checkbox that gets forgotten.

  • Adults only. Accounts are for adults (18 or older, or the age of majority where you live) acting as club staff — admins and coaches. Players and children do not have accounts and must not be given one.
  • Acting for a club. If you register or manage a club, you confirm that you are authorised to act for it and to accept these terms on its behalf.
  • Your account. Give accurate information, keep your login to yourself, and tell us if you think someone else is using your account. You are responsible for what happens under it. One person, one account; roles (admin, coach) and team scopes are set by your club's admins.

A3.Your club's responsibilities — this matters

PlayPivot is used by clubs all over the world, and data protection law isn't the same everywhere. PlayPivot doesn't decide which law applies to you: the laws of your own country do. By using PlayPivot for your club, you confirm that:

  • your club has a valid legal basis under the law that applies to it (for example the club's registration process, a parent's or guardian's consent where required, or a legitimate interest) to hold each player's data, and players and their families have been informed about it;
  • you only enter data your club needs to run its teams, and you do not enter sensitive data — health or injury details, religion, ethnicity, ID or social-security numbers (such as the Swiss AVS number), photos, home addresses, phone numbers or similar;
  • you will deal with requests from players or parents (access, correction, deletion) and keep what you enter accurate;
  • assessments, objectives, session feedback and comments about players — especially minors — are factual and respectful, and follow your club's own policy.

PlayPivot does not verify any of this — the same way a spreadsheet or a paper folder never did.

Two examples of what “the law that applies to you” can look like: the EU's GDPR generally requires parental consent for a child's data below age 16 (countries can lower this to 13); Switzerland's data protection act (nLPD) treats a child's own consent as valid only once the child has capacity of judgement (usually around 13), with a parent or guardian deciding below that. These are examples, not a summary of every country's law.

A4.Acceptable use — what's allowed, what isn't

Allowed

  • Adding data about players you or your club actually coach or manage.
  • Sharing your own exercises, plays and content with your team, your club association or the wider PlayPivot community, at whatever circle you choose.
  • Using another club's or coach's shared content within PlayPivot, the way it is meant to be used — for your own coaching.

Not allowed

  • Adding data about people you have no right to hold data about (see A3), or entering sensitive data.
  • Content that is unlawful, defamatory, discriminatory, harassing or demeaning towards players, parents, coaches, referees or anyone else.
  • Copying another club's shared content and republishing it outside PlayPivot as if it were your own, or sharing material you have no right to share (for example exercises copied from a paid course, book or video).
  • Using PlayPivot as general-purpose storage, for advertising, or for anything unrelated to coaching a team.
  • Trying to access another club's private data or another account without authorisation; probing, attacking, scraping or overloading the service; or interfering with its security.
  • Reselling or sub-licensing PlayPivot, or building a competing service from its content.

We may suspend or remove an account or content that breaks these terms — particularly anything touching a minor's data inappropriately — and we may report unlawful activity to the authorities.

A5.Your content, and what you allow others to do with it

You keep ownership of what you create (exercises, plays, sessions, notes…). You give PlayPivot a non-exclusive, worldwide, royalty-free licence to store, process, display and back up that content, only as needed to run the service for you and in line with the “Who can see it?” setting you choose.

  • Sharing beyond your club. If you set content to “My club association” or “PlayPivot Community”, you also give the clubs that can see it a non-exclusive, worldwide, royalty-free licence to view it, to copy it into their own library and to use it for their coaching inside PlayPivot. Copies already made stay with those clubs even if you later make the original private or delete it — treat anything you share beyond your club as shared for good.
  • Your name appears as the author next to content you share beyond your club.
  • Your responsibility. You confirm that you have the right to share what you share. If you believe something on PlayPivot infringes your rights, tell us (see Contact) with enough detail to find it, and we will review it and, where appropriate, remove it.
  • Private by design. Session feedback (ratings, comments, quick questions) and season objectives are visible only to your club — they are never shared with an association or the community.

A6.AI chats and imports — you are in control, and you are responsible

PlayPivot does not call any AI service. “Fill in with AI” only prepares a text prompt. If you paste it — together with a photo, PDF or Excel file — into an AI chat (ChatGPT, Claude or any other), that is a transfer you make yourself, to a third party outside PlayPivot, under that third party's own terms.

  • A match sheet usually contains players' names and jersey numbers (often minors). Only send what you are allowed to send, and prefer not to send more than needed — for example, crop or cover what isn't necessary.
  • AI can misread or invent data. Review everything before importing it. PlayPivot is not responsible for AI mistakes, or for what happens to data you send to an AI provider.
  • Excel files and pasted text you import are processed in your browser and then saved as your club's data, like anything else you type.

Clauses that by their nature continue — liability, governing law, and the licences for copies already made — survive the end of your use.

Part B — Privacy Policy

P1.Who is responsible for what

The chain of responsibility is the same wherever you are:

Player / family → Club → PlayPivot → Supabase and Infomaniak (providers)

  • Your club is the controller of the sporting data it enters — rosters, assessments, attendance, match statistics, training and season plans. The club decides which players to register, what to record and why, and must have a valid basis to do so (see A3).
  • PlayPivot is the processor of that same data: we process it on your club's instructions, to provide the service, and we do not sell it or use it for advertising or for our own unrelated purposes.
  • PlayPivot is the controller of a narrower set of data, for its own purposes: your own account details, security and technical logs, the acceptance log, support messages and anonymous usage statistics (P2).
  • Supabase and Infomaniak are our providers (sub-processors), under their own data-processing terms with us (P4).

Controller contact. For the data PlayPivot controls itself, and for any question about this policy: , .

P2.What we process, and what we don't

We try to collect the minimum needed to run a team. Concretely:

  • Account data (PlayPivot controls): email, name, a password protected by our authentication provider (we never see it in clear), role and team scope in your club, language, and the date and version of your acceptance of these terms.
  • Club data (club controls): for each player a first and last name, and optionally birth year, category, gender and a licence reference number; assessments (technique, game intelligence, personality, attitude); attendance; match statistics, match sheets and opponent notes; training sessions, comments and session feedback; season objectives and plans.
  • Content and files: exercises, plays, drawings, and your club's logo (stored in the file storage of our database provider).
  • Technical data: IP address and browser information, which our hosting and database providers' servers record in ordinary logs for security and operation; messages you send us through “Contact us”.
  • Anonymous usage statistics: see P9.
  • Occasional product emails only if you opt in: a couple a year about major new features, never sold or shared, and you can opt out at any time.

What we do not collect: player photos, scanned documents or licences, national identification numbers (such as the Swiss AVS number), health or injury data, addresses or phone numbers of players or parents, payment data (the service is free), or any data from advertising trackers. Please do not type such data into free-text fields (see A3).

P3.Why we process it, and on what basis

  • To provide the service you asked for (account, club data, sharing settings): performance of the agreement you accept in Part A.
  • Security, abuse prevention and keeping proof of acceptance: our legitimate interest in running a safe service and being able to show which terms you accepted.
  • Understanding overall usage to improve PlayPivot: legitimate interest, with anonymised data only (P9).
  • Product emails: your consent, withdrawable at any time.
  • Legal obligations where they apply to us.

We do not use your data to profile you, for automated decisions with legal effect, or for advertising. The “assessments” in the app are scores that coaches enter; PlayPivot does not generate evaluations of people.

P4.Who else handles your data, and transfers abroad

PlayPivot does not run its own servers. It relies on the following providers, under contracts that limit what they can do with the data. We do not sell data to anyone.

  • Supabase — database, authentication and file storage: this is where all the data described in P2 is held. Supabase is a company based in the United States; our database project is hosted in .
  • Infomaniak — web hosting of the application and of our self-hosted usage statistics. Its published documentation states that its data centres are located and operated in Switzerland.
  • jsDelivr (content delivery network) — the app loads some open-source code libraries from this public network. When your browser fetches them, the network sees your IP address and browser details, like any website you visit. No club or player data is sent to it.

If PlayPivot adds a provider that can access personal data (email delivery, analytics, AI features), it is added to this list before it goes live. We do not quietly add data-touching providers.

Transfers abroad. Because Supabase is a US company and your club may be anywhere in the world, personal data can be accessed from or processed in countries without the same level of data protection as Switzerland or the EU. Where that happens we rely on the safeguards recognised by Swiss and EU law — such as the provider's data-processing agreement with standard contractual clauses, or an adequacy decision — and on technical measures (encryption in transit and at rest). You can ask us for a copy of the safeguards (see Contact).

AI chats are not a provider of ours. If you paste a prompt and a match sheet into an AI chat, you are sending data to that AI company yourself (A6).

P5.Who can see what inside PlayPivot

Nothing is shared more widely than you choose. Most things you create have a “Who can see it?” setting:

  • Only me — private; not even your own teammates see it.
  • Whole club (or a team) — people active in your club, depending on their role.
  • My club association — a small group of clubs that agreed to share; needs approval.
  • PlayPivot Community — every club on PlayPivot can see it (view only), with your name as author.

Only exercises and plays can go beyond your club. Player data, assessments, attendance, match statistics, trainings, session feedback and season objectives stay inside your club (or the private/team circle you pick) and are never shown to an association or the community.

PlayPivot staff do not browse club data as part of normal operation; technical administrators can access the database only to run, secure and repair the service, to answer a request from you, or where the law requires.

P6.How long we keep data, and deletion

  • While your club is active we keep club data so the club can use it. The club decides what to record, and can delete players, sessions and other entries at any time. Removing a player from the roster removes their record and what is tied to it (assessments, attendance, match statistics).
  • Your account: Settings → “Delete my account & data” lets you leave any club at once (its data stays for the other members), or delete a club entirely if you are its only admin, which removes everything in it immediately. Deleting the login itself (email and password) needs a person on our side to complete, because the app cannot do that on its own for security reasons; the same page sends that request to us, and we aim to complete it within one month.
  • Content you shared beyond your club (exercises, plays) stays with the clubs that copied it (A5); we can delete, on request, anything you created that was never shared with anyone else.
  • Backups and logs. Our providers keep technical backups and server logs for a limited period set by their own policies; deleted data disappears from them when they are overwritten. The acceptance log is kept as proof of what was agreed.

P7.Your rights

Depending on the law that applies to you (for example the Swiss nLPD or the EU GDPR), you or — for a child — a parent or guardian can ask for: a copy of the data held about you; correction of inaccurate data; deletion; restriction or objection to certain processing; delivery of the data in a usable format; and withdrawal of any consent you gave. For club data, the quickest route is your club, which controls it and can usually resolve this from Settings; PlayPivot provides the technical means and will help the club respond.

For the data PlayPivot controls itself, write to us (see Contact). We aim to answer within one month, or within whatever shorter period your law requires. We may need to verify your identity first. You also have the right to complain to a data-protection authority — in Switzerland the Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner (FDPIC); in the EU, the authority of your country of residence.

P8.Data about minors

Most players in a youth club are minors, so this matters more here than in most software. Accounts are only for adults. Player assessments and objectives are evaluative data about a minor: follow your club's policy and keep them factual. Entering a minor's data may legally require a parent's or guardian's consent depending on where your club and its players are based (see A3) — obtaining it is the club's responsibility. A parent or guardian can ask the club, or us, to see, correct or delete everything held about their child (P7). We never use minors' data for advertising or profiling.

P9.Cookies, local storage and statistics

  • Strictly necessary: your login session and a few remembered preferences (such as column choices or language) are stored in your browser. This is needed for the service to work and is not used to track you.
  • Offline cache: a service worker keeps copies of the app's own files in your browser so it loads faster. It does not store your club data for other purposes.
  • Usage statistics: we use Matomo, self-hosted on our own Infomaniak infrastructure, to see overall usage (which pages are visited, roughly how often). It is configured without cookies and with anonymised IP addresses, so it does not build a profile of you or follow you across other sites, and nothing goes to a third-party analytics company. We use no advertising trackers.

P10.Security and incidents

Data is protected with row-level access rules: the database itself, not only the app's interface, enforces who can read or write what, based on role in the club. Connections are encrypted, and passwords are handled by our authentication provider's standard login system — we never see them. No system is perfectly secure, and we cannot guarantee that nothing will ever go wrong.

  • A security incident inside PlayPivot's own systems: we investigate, contain it, and notify affected clubs and, where the law requires, the authority.
  • An incident at a provider (P4): we coordinate with that provider and pass on what clubs need to know, as soon as we can.
  • If you find a security problem, please tell us (see Contact) before making it public, and do not access data that is not yours.

P11.For clubs and associations

If your club needs a written data-processing agreement with PlayPivot (for example because your federation, school or local law requires one), ask us and we will provide one. A club that works with the data of minors should in any case keep its own short privacy notice for players and families, saying what it records and in which tools; this page can be linked from it.
