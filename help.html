Help

A quick map of everything PlayPivot can do — one line per module, nothing more.

This page is deliberately brief: just enough to know what each module is for and where to find it. Want a proper step-by-step guide for one of these? Just ask in the app and we'll build a detailed walkthrough for that module — one at a time.

Day to day

Dashboard

Your home screen: your club, your role, quick stats, pending requests and messages, all in one place.

Open Dashboard →

Roster

Manage your players and staff — profiles, positions, seasons and teams — for the whole club in one table.

Open Roster →

Attendance

Track who showed up to training and games, session by session, so you always know who's available.

Open Attendance →

Match day

Prepare games, set your lineup, track live stats and record the final result and box score.

Open Match day →

Training & coaching content

Exercise creator

Build a drill from scratch with a court diagram, step-by-step instructions and coaching notes.

Open Exercise creator →

Exercise library

Browse, search and organize every drill you've created or that's been shared with you or your club.

Open Exercise library →

Training builder

Assemble a full practice session out of drills from your library, with timings, in the order you'll run it.

Open Training builder →

Play design

Draw offensive sets, defensive schemes and out-of-bounds plays on a court, and save them to your playbook.

Open Play design →

Play library

Browse every play across your club — every team's playbook, plus anything shared by other clubs — filtered by team, playbook or who made it.

Open Play library →

Player development

Player assessment

Score each player on technical skills, basketball IQ and attitude, and follow how they develop over time.

Open Player assessment →

Stats & Compare

See team and player performance over the season, and compare match statistics and trends side by side.

Open Stats & Compare →

Your club

Settings

Your club's logo, members and roles, sharing defaults, club associations, export language and account options.
