Welcome to PlayPivot 👋

One thing worth knowing before you dive in: almost everything you create — exercises, plays, sessions, matches, assessments — has its own "Who can see it?" setting. Nothing is shared further than you choose.

🔒Only me — private, not even your own teammates see it.
👥My team — everyone active in your club (this is the default).
🤝My club association — a small group of clubs who agreed to share, needs approval.
🌐PlayPivot Community — every club on PlayPivot can see it (view only).

If you're a club admin, Settings also lets you set all of a type at once (e.g. every exercise), and manage who's in your club and their role (Team admin or Coach).

Worth knowing too: only add data about players you actually coach, and you're responsible for having the right to hold it (a guardian's consent, especially for a minor — see Privacy & Terms). Don't republish someone else's shared content outside PlayPivot as your own.
