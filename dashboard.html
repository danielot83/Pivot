Welcome to Pivot 👋

One thing worth knowing before you dive in: almost everything you create — exercises, plays, sessions, matches, assessments — has its own "Who can see it?" setting. Nothing is shared further than you choose.

🔒Only me — private, not even your own teammates see it.
👥My team — everyone active in your club (this is the default).
🤝My club association — a small group of clubs who agreed to share, needs approval.
🌐Pivot Community — every club on Pivot can see it (view only).

If you're a club admin, Settings also lets you set all of a type at once (e.g. every exercise), manage who's in your club and their role, and — for players specifically — link their account to their roster entry so they can see what's meant for them, and nothing more.
