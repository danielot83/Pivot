— nothing is pre-placed. Pick "Attacker", "Defender", "Ball", "Cone", "Coach", "Post", or "Handoff" and click the court to add one; you'll be asked for a number/label for attackers and defenders.
Move/select
— drag any point you've placed. Double-click a player/defender to rename it, or double-click any other point to remove it. Clicking a line/screen/zone (or a point) selects it — then
(or Delete/Backspace on a keyboard) removes just that one, without touching anything else on the step. Drawing a new line leaves that drawing tool active (so you can draw several in a row) — switch back to
first if you want to click and delete something you just drew; "Delete element" tells you if nothing is selected yet instead of doing nothing.
Steps
— click "+ Add step" as many times as the play needs. Each new step starts with points positioned exactly where the previous step's cuts/dribbles/screens ended — draw a pass from 1 to 2 and step 2 opens with the ball already at 2; draw a screen and the screener carries over positioned right where they set it, not where they started.
Options (branches)
— when a play can go one way or another depending on what the defense does, click "🔀 Split into options here" on the play's last step. The first option (V1) keeps the play's usual black — nothing changes color yet; the second option (V2) onward each gets its own distinct color, so the moment a play actually forks into something different, the color change is obvious. Each option has its own mini-sequence of steps (reusing everything above), reached through the tabs that appear — its lines/screens/zones are drawn in that option's color automatically. An option can branch again into its own sub-options (a "variant of the variant") the same way, from ITS last step — a sub-option shades its parent's own color (lighter/darker) instead of a brand new one, so you can tell at a glance which family it belongs to. Once a play has options, "Download animation (GIF)" turns into two buttons: one GIF per option (each labelled "V1"/"V2"… in its own color, centered, with the play's name right underneath), or a single combined GIF with every option played in full, one after another, each labelled as it starts; "Export to PDF" prints every step of every option on its own page, breadcrumbed so you can tell which option you're reading.
Note for this step
— a short caption (e.g. "1 passes to 2 and cuts backdoor") that appears on screen while that step plays during "Animate", and under the diagram in the exported PDF.
This step's court
— half or full, chosen independently for every step.
Speed
— slows down or speeds up "Animate whole sequence"; the same speed also gets baked into the downloaded GIF.
Download animation (GIF)
— renders the whole sequence as a GIF file you can send or embed anywhere, not just play it here.
Undo
removes the last action, or the last point if there are no actions left.
Clear this step
wipes everything on the current step only.
